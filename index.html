PUBLICATIONMARCH 2026

Myocardial Perfusion Segmentation with PyTorch U-Net

This study explores deep learning-based segmentation of myocardial perfusion regions using a U-Net model. Medical images were preprocessed into 2D slices and normalized. A baseline U-Net with a pretrained ResNet34 encoder performed well, achieving a Dice score of ~0.86. However, a more complex tuned model underperformed, showing that segmentation is sensitive to small datasets and class imbalance. Overall, simpler models with strong preprocessing yielded the best results.

PyTorchU-NetMedical Imaging

Modeling Immune Response Gene Expression Using Two-Way ANOVA and Repeated Measures in SAS

This study analyzes immune response gene expression using Two-Way ANOVA and repeated-measures models in SAS on RNA-seq data from control and immune conditions. Results show that repeated-measures modeling detects a small but significant condition effect and substantial technical variability, highlighting the importance of accounting for correlated gene expression data.

Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic condition associated with elevated blood glucose and long-term health complications. Early screening can support preventive care, but predictive modeling remains challenging when medical features are noisy, incomplete, or imbalanced. This study evaluates baseline and optimized TensorFlow feedforward neural networks for binary diabetes prediction using the Pima Indians Diabetes dataset.

REPORTDECEMBER 2025

Lambda GPU Cloud Analytics

This project analyzes factors influencing GPU cloud pricing on Lambda using data from 300 instances. Statistical methods show that hardware performance, VRAM, reliability, and region have little impact on pricing. Instead, pricing is largely driven by customer segmentation, with enterprise and individual users paying more despite similar usage.
