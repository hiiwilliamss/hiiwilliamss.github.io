Publication

Myocardial Perfusion Segmentation with PyTorch U-Net

Research paper · March 2026

This study explores deep learning-based segmentation of myocardial perfusion regions using a U-Net model. Medical images were preprocessed into 2D slices and normalized. A baseline U-Net with a pretrained ResNet34 encoder performed well, achieving a Dice score of ~0.86. However, a more complex tuned model underperformed, showing that segmentation is sensitive to small datasets and class imbalance. Overall, simpler models with strong preprocessing yielded the best results.

TensorFlow MLP for Heart Disease Prediction

Research paper · March 2026

This project applies a TensorFlow-based MLP to predict heart disease using clinical and demographic data. It involves exploratory analysis, preprocessing (one-hot encoding and scaling), and building a binary classification model to identify patterns linked to heart disease.

Report

Lambda GPU Cloud Analytics

Team research · December 2025

This project analyzes factors influencing GPU cloud pricing on Lambda using data from 300 instances. Statistical methods show that hardware performance, VRAM, reliability, and region have little impact on pricing. Instead, pricing is largely driven by customer segmentation, with enterprise and individual users paying more despite similar usage.
